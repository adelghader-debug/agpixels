Common questions

Google Ads management, answered.

How much does Google Ads management cost?

AGPixels charges a flat monthly fee, not a percentage of your ad spend. Local Ads is $350 per month for accounts spending up to about $1,000 per month on ads. Growth Ads is $650 per month for accounts spending roughly $1,000 to $3,000 per month. Your ad budget itself is paid directly to Google, separate from this fee.

Why don't you charge a percentage of ad spend?

A percentage-of-spend fee means your management cost goes up every time your budget does, even if the work involved barely changes. It also means a small local business ends up paying an outsized fee relative to their actual ad budget. A flat fee is simpler, predictable, and does not penalize you for having a modest budget.

Is there a minimum ad budget?

There is no hard minimum, but Local Ads is built for accounts spending roughly $300 to $1,000 per month. Below that, there usually is not enough traffic for Google's algorithms to optimize campaigns effectively.

Who owns the Google Ads account?

You do. Your Google Ads account, conversion data, and campaign history stay in your name. If you ever move to a different provider, you take all of it with you.

Do you also build the website the ads point to?

Yes. Website design and Google Ads management are often most effective together, since a site built around what an ad promises tends to convert better than sending paid traffic to a generic homepage. You can hire AGPixels for either service on its own, or both together.
