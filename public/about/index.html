About AGPixels

The person behind your website.

AGPixels is a small, founder-led web design studio based in Ottawa. When you work with us, you work directly with me, Adel Ghader, from the first call to launch and every update after.

Meet Adel

Hi, I'm Adel.

I'm a web designer and developer based in Ottawa, and I started AGPixels with one simple idea: small businesses deserve websites that are actually built for them, not pulled off a shelf.

Before AGPixels, I watched too many owners pay for slow, generic sites and then get left on their own the moment something broke. So I do it the opposite way. Every site is custom built, the price is agreed in writing before any work starts, and there is one real person, me, who answers when you need a change.

Today AGPixels builds and maintains websites for trades, clinics, trainers, and service businesses across Ottawa, the GTA, and the rest of Canada. Some clients came for a single site and stayed for years of support. That is the relationship I am after: not a quick handoff, but a long partnership where your website keeps getting better.
