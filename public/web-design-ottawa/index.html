Common questions

Web design in Ottawa, answered.

How much does a website cost in Ottawa?

Our Ottawa web design packages start at $1,500 (Get Found), $3,500 (More Calls, our most popular), and $5,000 (Market Leader). Ongoing WordPress care starts at $150 per month. You receive a fixed price in writing before any work starts, and you only pay once you love the homepage design. For the full breakdown of what changes the price, see our website pricing guide.

How long does it take to build a website?

Most 5 to 8 page small business websites launch in about 4 weeks from kickoff. Redesigns are often faster because the content already exists. You get a clear timeline in your proposal.

Do you meet clients in person?

Yes. AGPixels is based in Ottawa and works with clients across Ottawa, Kanata, Orleans, Nepean, Barrhaven, Stittsville, and Gatineau. We are happy to meet in person, on a call, or at your place of business.

Do you redesign existing websites?

Yes. We audit your current site, keep what works, fix what does not, and rebuild it on a fast, mobile-first foundation. Your content is migrated and improved, not thrown away.

Who owns the website when it is done?

You do. The domain, hosting, code, and content are all in your name from day one. There is no lock-in and no proprietary platform holding your site hostage.
