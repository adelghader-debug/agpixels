Patient app and website: proposed improvements, before and after

Every "before" is today's app or website running on the Klinika test server with test clinics, test doctors and a test patient. Every "after" is the proposed design using the same data. Please mark each item in the approval table at the end.

What changes, in short

Show what patients need to decideEvery doctor and clinic card shows the title, specialty, clinic, rating with its number of reviews, the next free time and the price.
Clear booking stepsReason, date and time, review and pay, with a step bar. The booking is made only when the patient confirms and pays.
Wallet in the appPatients can pay with their Klinika wallet in the app, as they already can on the website.
One status on every visitEach visit says if it is confirmed, unpaid or waiting for the patient, with the one action that fits.
One calm design for app and websiteOne set of colours, type sizes, spacing, cards and buttons, used the same way on every screen.

Already fixed in version 1.6.1

These are built, tested and part of the Android update being released now.

✓

Buttons hidden on newer Android phones

On Android 15 and later, phones with back, home and recent buttons hid Proceed and other bottom buttons. Pages now stop above that bar.

✓

Profile menu

Rows now share one icon size and margin, and show an arrow. The list no longer scrolls under the clock.

✓

Empty pages

"No items found" and blank pages are replaced with clear messages in English and Arabic.

Patient app

In the order a patient meets them: home, finding a doctor, booking, visits, offers, laboratory and nursing.

1

Home

Today

  • Heavy purple page with a large logo and empty space at the top.
  • Your next visit is not shown, so patients open Visits to check it.
  • Offers sit at the very bottom.

Proposed

  • Calm white page that greets the patient by name.
  • Your next visit at the top, with its time and what happens next.
  • Services as clear tiles with one line each: video call, at the clinic, at home.
  • Instant consultation and offers below, smaller.
2

Specialties

Today

  • A grid of icons and names only.
  • No sign of how many doctors each specialty has.

Proposed

  • Two columns with the number of doctors under each specialty.
  • Search also finds specialties by symptom.
  • Needs a small server change: the server counts the doctors but does not send the number to the app.
3

Doctor list

Today

  • Name, one specialty label, price and a star only.
  • No title, clinic, number of reviews or free time.

Proposed

  • Title and specialty, clinic, rating with its number of reviews, a Licensed mark.
  • Next free time and the price per visit at the bottom of each card.
  • Already built in the app and running on the test server: this is a real screenshot.
4

Clinic list

Today

  • Clinic name, district and rating only.
  • Patients cannot see which services the clinic offers or what it costs.

Proposed

  • Address, rating with reviews and how many doctors of this specialty work there.
  • Services offered (clinic, video, home) as small labels.
  • Next free time and the lowest price.
5

Visit reason

Today

  • Long instruction in large type.
  • Recording and attaching look like the main actions.
  • No sign of which doctor is being booked or how many steps are left.

Proposed

  • The chosen doctor at the top and a three step bar: reason, date and time, review and pay.
  • A short question with a hint below it.
  • Voice note and files as secondary buttons.
6

Date and time

Today

  • Strong purple and pink blocks, small day boxes.
  • "This doctor available in few clinics" shows even with one clinic.
  • Days off look like normal days until tapped.

Proposed

  • Each day shows how many free times it has, and days off say so.
  • Times grouped into afternoon and evening.
  • The chosen time and price stay visible above the Continue button.
  • Times are marked as Saudi time.
7

Review and pay

Today

  • The booking is created when the patient taps Book now, before this page.
  • Long list of labels: the specialty shown twice, zero fees and zero VAT listed.
  • Card is the only payment method in the app, even with money in the wallet.

Proposed

  • One clear summary: who, when, where.
  • Only the price lines that apply, then the total.
  • Pay with the Klinika wallet or a card. The wallet shows its balance.
  • The clinic's cancellation rule in one line.
  • The booking is created only when the patient taps Confirm and pay.
8

Booking confirmed

Today

  • Not shown here: it only appears after a real paid booking, which this review did not make.

Proposed

  • A clear confirmation with the visit details, how it was paid and the booking number.
  • Add to calendar and Directions buttons.
9

My visits

Today

  • Large cards with a lot of empty space.
  • The countdown box and arrow are hard to understand.
  • Unpaid, confirmed and waiting visits look alike.

Proposed

  • A status label on each visit: confirmed, waiting for your reply, unpaid.
  • Date and time on the right, where the eye looks first.
  • One action per visit that fits its status: accept a new time, open the call, get directions.
10

Visit details

Today

  • Long list of labels and values.
  • Test names cut off.
  • Status shown at the bottom.

Proposed

  • Status, date and time at the top in large type.
  • Each test with its price, and what was paid.
  • The cancellation deadline in plain words.
  • Reschedule and Cancel always at the bottom.
11

Visit report

Today

  • Four large headings even when the doctor wrote nothing.
  • "The doctor did not add the notes" repeated.

Proposed

  • Only the sections the doctor filled in.
  • Notes, recommendations and prescriptions in calm cards.
  • Book follow-up and Download PDF at the bottom.
12

Offers

Today

  • The green bar does not explain itself.
  • No clinic or doctor on the card.

Proposed

  • Clinic and doctor on each offer.
  • The saving as a label on the photo, and "Ends in 60 days" in words.
  • Category chips to filter quickly.
13

Offer details

Today

  • Specialty and clinic as a plain list.
  • The price only on the next screen.

Proposed

  • Photo, saving and end date at the top.
  • The doctor and the clinic as cards.
  • Price and next free time next to the Book offer button.
14

Laboratory

Today

  • Home or lab is chosen in a pop-up before this page.
  • Categories as grey rows with no counts or prices.

Proposed

  • Home or lab as a switch on the page.
  • Popular packages first, with the number of tests and the starting price.
  • Categories with the number of tests and the lowest price.
  • A bar at the bottom shows what is selected and the total.
15

Nursing

Today

  • Tasks as large rows with round buttons.
  • No prices until later.

Proposed

  • Each service with a short description and its starting price.
  • Clear tick boxes, with the selected count and total at the bottom.
16

Address for home visits

Today

  • The address shows as a map code, for example "Riyadh RJGQ+RWC".
  • No place for the floor, apartment or a note for the provider.

Proposed

  • The street and district in words.

Problems found during the review

Found while walking every journey with test data. Each will be fixed with the screen it belongs to.

ProblemWhat happens
Calendar never loads for some doctorsWhen a doctor's hours are not saved for all seven days, the date page shows a spinner forever. 25 doctor and service combinations on the test server have this.
Booking is made before the reviewTapping Book now on the date page already creates the booking, unpaid, before the patient sees the price summary.
No wallet payment in the appThe wallet can only be used on the website. The app offers card payment only.
Next free time a day early abroadFor a phone outside Saudi time, the server returns the next free day one day early.
Address shown as a map codeHome visit addresses show as a code such as "Riyadh RJGQ+RWC" instead of a street.
Wrong text with one clinic"This doctor available in few clinics" shows when the doctor works at one clinic.
Repeated and empty linesThe booking summary lists the specialty twice and shows fees and VAT of 0 SAR.

Design rules behind the "after" screens

One set of rules for the app, the website and later the staff dashboards. Today the app alone uses 55 different colours typed directly into screens, and the dashboard uses a different pink from the app.

Pink
Buttons and actions only
Purple
Brand accents, titles, next free time
Ink
Headings and key text
Background
Pages; cards are white
Green
Confirmed, paid, licensed
Amber
Waiting for the patient
Red
Cancelled, failed, cancel buttons
  • Type: the app's own Bahij font, in four sizes (titles 18 to 22, names 16, body 14, small 12).
  • Spacing: 16 on every page edge, 12 between cards, 14 inside cards.
  • Cards: white, rounded 16, a thin line and a soft shadow. Prices and next free time in a shaded footer.
  • Buttons: one pink button per screen for the main action; other actions as outlined buttons.
  • Every list has a clear message when empty, and every button that saves something shows that it is working.
  • Arabic is laid out right to left with the same rules, and numbers stay in Latin digits.

Small server changes needed

Most of the work is in the app and website. These need small additions on the server, using data it already holds:

  • Specialty list: send the number of doctors (the server already counts them).
  • Clinic list: send the services offered, the lowest price and the next free time.
  • Laboratory: send the number of tests and the lowest price per category.
  • Home visits: turn the map point into a street address.
  • Next free time: fix the date for phones outside Saudi time.
