Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 26, 2026

AGPixels ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have about it.

This policy is written to comply with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL).

1. Who we are

AGPixels is a Canadian web design, web maintenance, and mobile application development service operated by Adel Ghader. You can reach us at hello@agpixels.ca.

2. Information we collect

We collect only the information needed to respond to your enquiry and run our website:

  • Information you give us — when you submit our contact form, we collect your name, email address, project type, and any details you include in your message.
  • Information collected automatically — when you visit this site, our analytics provider (see Section 4) may collect anonymous usage data such as your approximate geographic region (country / city), device type, browser, pages visited, and the source that referred you to the site (e.g. Google search, an ad, a direct link).
  • Cookies — see Section 5.

3. How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry and provide a quote or proposal.
  • To follow up about your project, with your consent.
  • To understand which marketing channels are working (anonymous, aggregated only).
  • To detect and prevent spam or abuse of our contact form.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

4. Third-party service providers

We use a small number of trusted services to run this site. Each receives only the minimum information required:

  • Cloudflare — hosting, DNS, and email forwarding for our domain. Cloudflare Privacy Policy
  • Web3Forms — handles the contact form submission and forwards it to our email. Web3Forms Privacy Policy
  • Google Analytics 4 & Google Ads — anonymous traffic analytics and advertising performance measurement. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Fonts — serves the Inter typeface used on this site. Google Privacy Policy

5. Cookies and similar technologies

This site uses cookies for two purposes:

  • Essential — required for the site to function (e.g. remembering your privacy preferences). These cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics & advertising — set by Google Analytics and Google Ads to measure traffic and ad performance. You can opt out by installing the Google Analytics Opt-Out Browser Add-On or by adjusting your browser's cookie settings.

6. How long we keep your information

Contact form submissions are retained in our email inbox for as long as needed to respond to your enquiry and complete any resulting work, plus a reasonable period afterward for our business records (typically up to seven years for tax purposes). Analytics data is retained for the default Google Analytics 4 retention period (currently 14 months).

7. Your rights

Under PIPEDA, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we hold about you.
  • Access a copy of that information.
  • Ask us to correct inaccuracies.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time, subject to legal or contractual restrictions.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at hello@agpixels.ca and we'll respond within 30 days.

8. CASL — anti-spam

If you submit our contact form, you are giving express consent to receive replies and follow-up communications about your enquiry. Every email we send will include our contact information and an unsubscribe option.

9. Children

This website is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information: HTTPS encryption in transit, reputable third-party providers, and access controls on our email and form data. No system is 100% secure, but we treat your data with care.

11. Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, the new version will be posted here with a revised "Last updated" date at the top.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email hello@agpixels.ca.
