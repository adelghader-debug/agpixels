Emotion focused therapy

An 8 to 20 session approach for individuals, couples and families that works on emotional awareness and regulation.

For
Individuals, couples and families
Format
In clinic; a short-term plan of 8 to 20 sessions
To start
Call to book counselling and ask for EFT.

Emotion focused therapy (EFT) is a short-term treatment plan, typically 8 to 20 sessions, that helps clients improve problematic emotional states or relationships. It is based on the premise that emotions are at the root of unhelpful patterns, self-talk and relationship problems, and it works for individuals, couples and families.

What it works on

  • Awareness of your current emotional state
  • Emotional regulation and the ability to self-balance
  • How emotions transform and change from moment to moment

Three phases

  1. Assess and de-escalate. Uncover the underlying emotional problem through detailed assessment and consultation.
  2. Change events. Replace negative emotional patterns with new emotional experiences and learn to communicate them more effectively.
  3. Consolidation. Resolve old emotional problems and build an actionable plan for continued success outside the clinic.

For couples, EFT helps partners understand themselves and each other, de-escalate conflict and keep disagreements constructive. For families, it helps each member understand their own emotions and those of others, and communicate them in more helpful ways.
