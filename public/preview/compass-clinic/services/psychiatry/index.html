Psychiatry

Medical assessment, diagnosis and treatment, by referral from your family doctor and covered by MSP.

For
Children, youth and adults with a doctor's referral
Format
In clinic; MSP-covered
To start
Ask your family doctor for a referral. Once we receive it, we contact you to book.
Not accepting new clients. Psychiatry at Compass Clinic is currently closed to new referrals. We will update this page when that changes.

Psychiatry is the medical specialty devoted to the diagnosis, prevention and treatment of mental disorders. Psychiatrists are trained to assess both the mental and physical aspects of psychological problems.

Referral

Psychiatry is one of the few services at Compass Clinic that requires a doctor's referral, usually from your general practitioner, because psychiatric assessment is covered through MSP. Once a referral is received, we contact you to book.

What it includes

  • Consultation to learn about you or your child and your needs
  • Assessment by the psychiatry team, which may also draw on psychologists, occupational therapists and speech-language pathologists
  • Diagnosis, made carefully after in-depth assessment and diagnostic clarification
  • Treatment options discussed with you so you can make an informed choice, which may include individual or group counselling, medication management, school planning, psychoeducation, family therapy and support groups
  • Education and family support, because so much of a child's life happens at home and school
