Group counselling

Small groups that meet regularly with a therapist, such as sibling groups or anxiety groups for teens.

For
Children, youth and families
Format
In clinic; sessions run from one hour to two or three hours depending on the group
To start
Call to ask which groups are running.

Group counselling is where a small group of clients or families meets regularly with a therapist to improve communication and foster growth. Groups usually focus on education and cohesion, and they help psychological health in a social context. Members discuss issues and offer feedback to each other with the therapist's guidance. All personal information is confidential.

Complex diagnoses often affect the whole family. We offer support that targets specific groups or concerns, for example sibling groups or anxiety groups for teens.

Is it right for you?

Many people are reluctant at first, usually because of social anxiety or the thought of sharing personal details. Most open up after experiencing the support other members provide. Not everyone needs to share as much as others; you can gain and give support by being there and listening.
