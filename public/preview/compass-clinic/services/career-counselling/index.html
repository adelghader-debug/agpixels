Career counselling

Psychometric testing plus a plan, offered by Registered Clinical Counsellors and psychologists.

For
Youth and adults at any stage of a career
Format
In clinic
To start
Call to book. Most extended health plans cover the counsellors and psychologists who provide it.

Career counselling helps with choosing, changing or leaving a career. It has two goals: identify potential options given your skills, interests and experience, and devise a plan to reach your career-related goal. It is available at any stage of life.

Our approach

We think the usual question, "what do you want to be?", suggests there is one answer. Most people change career paths more than once. The better question is "what makes what you offer special?" That is a talent lens on assessment, and it is helpful not only for career planning but also for scholarship and college interviews.

We do psychometric testing that defines career strengths, stretches and preferred workplace settings. We also help you define your story, because people remember stories. And we treat life as a balance between love, work, play and health, so that a passion that does not fit in the work column can still have its place.

Career counselling at Compass Clinic is provided by Registered Clinical Counsellors or psychologists. Most people have extended health coverage for these services.
