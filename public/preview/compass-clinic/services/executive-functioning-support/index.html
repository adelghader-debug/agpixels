Executive functioning support

Tutoring and coaching in planning, organization, time management and self-regulation, supervised by Registered Psychologists.

For
Students of all ages and achievement levels
Format
In person or by video with student clinicians; by video only with the Clinical Nurse
To start
Call to book. A 10-week group with individual coaching is offered periodically for post-secondary students.

Executive function is the part of the brain that acts as a manager: planning, organization, time management, and the self-regulation of attention, emotion and behaviour. Many students struggle at school because of difficulties with it. They lose things, struggle to complete and hand in work, and often have trouble with written output.

How a tutor helps

Our EF tutors are student clinicians supervised by Registered Psychologists. They help with homework planning and completion, study skills, organization, time management, written papers and motivation. Support is individualized and combines behavioural and technological strategies. Student clinicians are available in person (not for all age groups) and by video or phone.

A Clinical Nurse with specialized EF training also provides executive-functioning support. The nurse works independently and provides services exclusively by video conference.

Group and coaching

The clinic periodically runs a 10-week executive functioning group for post-secondary students that includes individual, supportive coaching. Ask whether a session is coming up.
