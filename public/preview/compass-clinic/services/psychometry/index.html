Psychometry

Standardized testing by psychometrists, working with Registered Psychologists, for ages 2 to 18.

For
Children and youth, ages 2 to 18
Format
In clinic; observations may also happen at home, daycare or school
To start
Part of an assessment booked through the clinic.

Children with medical, physical and developmental delays often have unique learning needs that affect school and therapy. Our psychometrists provide comprehensive testing as part of psychoeducational assessments for clients aged 2 to 18, helping identify strengths and challenges and develop learning strategies.

Psychometrists have specialized graduate-level training in administering standardized tests, and work in collaboration with Registered Psychologists.

What our psychometrists do

  • Screen new clients aged 2 to 5 with developmental concerns
  • Review previous reports to inform the current assessment
  • Observe clients in natural environments such as home, daycare and school
  • Meet with parents to discuss learning strategies and needs
  • Discuss approaches to managing specific learning, developmental and behavioural disabilities
  • Liaise with the consulting psychologist and refer to other agencies
  • Support school advocacy and community consultation
