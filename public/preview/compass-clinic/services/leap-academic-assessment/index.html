LEAP academic assessment

A follow-up for existing clients that updates the academic testing from a psychoeducational assessment.

For
Children and youth who have had a psychoeducational assessment at Compass Clinic
Format
In clinic, about 1.5 to 2 hours of testing with a psychometrist
To start
Call to book, usually about one year after the original assessment.

The Learning Education Assessment Program (LEAP) is designed for clients who have already completed a psychoeducational assessment with Compass Clinic. It updates the academic portion of the testing to review school progress and provide new recommendations. It suits families with specific concerns about learning who want a follow-up.

How it is structured

A session with the psychologist reviews how the child has been doing since the original assessment. The child then completes academic testing with a psychometrist, which takes approximately 1.5 to 2 hours. A feedback session with parents follows to review results and recommendations.

How often

Our general recommendation is about one year after the psychoeducational assessment. Some families choose a yearly academic check-up. We do not complete these more than once a year for a child. A LEAP assessment is not a substitute for a psychoeducational assessment; it requires one to have been completed first.
