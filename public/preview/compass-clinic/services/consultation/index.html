Consultation

A one-hour meeting with a Registered Psychologist to work out what you need and plan next steps.

For
Individuals, families, schools and organizations
Format
In clinic; billed by the psychologist's time
To start
Call to book. Bring school records or previous assessments if you have them.

Many individuals and families find it difficult to know where to start and how to navigate assessment and treatment options. A consultation with a Registered Psychologist can be a helpful first step to establishing a plan of supports. The psychologist meets with you, your child and other important supports to identify needs and provide recommendations.

What it involves

Consultations are initially booked as a one-hour meeting. During the meeting a plan is developed, which may involve further follow-up. The psychologist reviews your concerns and relevant collateral information such as school records and previous assessments. Consultation is billed on the time spent by the psychologist, which can be discussed in the appointment.

Other kinds of consultation

  • Schools, medical, and child- and youth-focused organizations
  • Talks and workshops for professionals and caregivers
  • Case-specific consultations
