Services

Assessment, therapy and allied services for children, youth, parents and adults. Every service page says who it is for, how it is delivered and how to start.

Assessments

  • Autism assessmentDiagnostic assessment for children, youth and adults, following BCAAN standards, using the ADOS-2 and ADI-R.
  • ADHD assessment (children and youth)Questionnaire-based consultation or a full psychoeducational assessment that also maps learning needs.
  • Adult ADHD assessmentA video-based assessment with a Registered Psychologist, a feedback session and a written report.
  • Learning disability assessmentCompleted through a psychoeducational assessment of cognitive skills and academic skills in reading, math and writing.
  • Giftedness assessmentAssessment of intellectual giftedness to support school planning and a possible gifted designation.
  • Psychoeducational assessmentA detailed picture of how a child learns: intelligence, academic skills, attention, executive functioning, memory and behaviour.
  • LEAP academic assessmentA follow-up for existing clients that updates the academic testing from a psychoeducational assessment.
  • PsychometryStandardized testing by psychometrists, working with Registered Psychologists, for ages 2 to 18.

Therapy and counselling

  • Counselling and therapyIndividual, couple and family counselling with Registered Clinical Counsellors and Registered Psychologists.
  • Cognitive behaviour therapyA short-term, structured approach that works on the connection between thoughts, emotions and behaviours.
  • Emotion focused therapyAn 8 to 20 session approach for individuals, couples and families that works on emotional awareness and regulation.
  • Group counsellingSmall groups that meet regularly with a therapist, such as sibling groups or anxiety groups for teens.
  • Career counsellingPsychometric testing plus a plan, offered by Registered Clinical Counsellors and psychologists.

Allied and support services

  • Occupational therapyAssessment and family-centred therapy for the everyday activities a child finds hard: self-care, school work, play.
  • Speech-language pathologyAssessment, diagnosis and treatment for speech, language and social communication.
  • Executive functioning supportTutoring and coaching in planning, organization, time management and self-regulation, supervised by Registered Psychologists.
  • ConsultationA one-hour meeting with a Registered Psychologist to work out what you need and plan next steps.
  • Whole Life supportFollow-up over the years for diagnoses made in childhood, at each transition and whenever it is needed.
  • Psychiatry Not accepting new clientsMedical assessment, diagnosis and treatment, by referral from your family doctor and covered by MSP.

Not sure where to start?

A one-hour consultation with a Registered Psychologist is the usual first step when the right assessment is not obvious. Or call and describe what is going on; that is what the first call is for.
