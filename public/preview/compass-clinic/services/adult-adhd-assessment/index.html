Adult ADHD assessment

A video-based assessment with a Registered Psychologist, a feedback session and a written report.

For
Adults
Format
Fully virtual, by video call
To start
Book online or call. You complete a consent form and questionnaires when you book.

Adult ADHD affects attention, planning, organization, memory and impulse control. Many people are not diagnosed until adulthood because their symptoms were overlooked or misunderstood, or because the coping strategies that worked at school stop working as responsibilities grow.

What the assessment involves

  1. Before the appointment. When you book, you receive an email with the ADHD questionnaires, the intake form and the link for your video call.
  2. Assessment appointment. A Registered Psychologist trained in ADHD assessment meets with you by video for about 1.5 to 2 hours, then observes you completing online tests of focus and attention. You need a private space, a computer or laptop and a reliable internet connection; a phone is not enough.
  3. Feedback. Within 4 to 6 weeks you are contacted to arrange a 30-minute feedback session where the psychologist explains the results. A full written report follows.

What it costs

The clinic publishes the fee for this assessment: a full assessment is $1,527.50, with a 50% deposit ($763.75) paid at booking. The deposit is refunded in full if the appointment is cancelled 3 business days in advance; a $352.50 charge applies otherwise. The balance is paid before feedback.

Good to know

  • Having an assessment does not guarantee a diagnosis. A diagnosis requires meeting the DSM-5 criteria.
  • No medications are prescribed at the clinic. If a diagnosis is made, the report outlines next steps, which may include a referral for medical consultation, therapy or coaching, groups, and lifestyle strategies.
