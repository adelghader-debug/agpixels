Counselling and therapy

Individual, couple and family counselling with Registered Clinical Counsellors and Registered Psychologists.

For
Children, youth, adults, couples and families
Format
In clinic; after-hours appointments available with Registered Clinical Counsellors
To start
Call to book. Tell us what you are looking for and we will suggest a fit.

Counselling, also called therapy, is offered by several Registered Clinical Counsellors and some Registered Psychologists to support children, youth, adults, couples and families experiencing difficulties personally, socially or psychologically. Our counsellors draw on their own education, training and experience, and many work in an integrated style that combines techniques from different approaches.

The first session

The first session is generally a chance for the counsellor or psychologist to collect background information and understand the concerns bringing you to therapy. It may also be the start of a plan to support you. The relationship you build is the foundation of the work: counselling is both an opportunity to talk, reflect and gain awareness, and a place to develop practical skills.

RCC or R.Psych?

Both may offer counselling or psychotherapy, and the quality may be similar. The RCC credential requires a master's degree and training in counselling. The R.Psych credential in BC requires a doctoral degree in psychology (or a master's degree with prior registration in another province), and a Registered Psychologist has training in both therapy and assessment, including diagnosis.

How long, how often

Some people find improvement with short-term therapy of 4 to 6 sessions; others stay longer. Most book weekly or biweekly and reduce frequency as they make gains.

Cost and coverage

The cost depends on the counsellor or psychologist providing the service; please ask about rates for a specific clinician. Counselling is not covered by MSP, but many extended health plans cover Registered Clinical Counsellors and Registered Psychologists. We do not bill insurers directly; a receipt is issued for you to submit.
