Giftedness assessment

Assessment of intellectual giftedness to support school planning and a possible gifted designation.

For
Children and youth
Format
In clinic
To start
Call to book. Completed as a psychoeducational assessment.

Giftedness describes an exceptional ability within a domain. At Compass Clinic we assess for intellectual giftedness, an intellectual ability significantly higher than average. Giftedness is not a diagnosis; school districts designate gifted students, and identifying giftedness supports the school's development of an Individual Education Plan.

How we assess

The assessment is completed through a psychoeducational assessment. Giftedness is determined by evaluating cognitive functioning, and it is just as important to assess how a child is performing academically. That shows whether a child is working to the potential suggested by their intellectual ability, and where they may need more challenge or more support. It is common for gifted children to have learning differences that meet the criteria for a learning disability.
