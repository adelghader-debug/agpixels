ADHD assessment (children and youth)

Questionnaire-based consultation or a full psychoeducational assessment that also maps learning needs.

For
Children and youth
Format
In clinic; also on outreach trips
To start
Call to book. Parent and teacher questionnaires are part of the process.

Children and youth with ADHD show a persistent pattern of inattention and/or hyperactivity that interferes with daily functioning and development. For a diagnosis, symptoms need to be evident in two or more settings and to have been present before the age of 12.

Two ways to assess

  • ADHD consultation. A clinical interview plus standardized questionnaires completed by parents and teachers.
  • Psychoeducational assessment. A more thorough route that also identifies learning strengths and needs, so the school has a learning plan to work from. See psychoeducational assessment.

Adults looking for their own assessment can read about adult ADHD assessment, which is offered by video.
