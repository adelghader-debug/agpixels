What happens when you call

The same four steps for every family, so you know what is coming before you pick up the phone.

1

You call or write

You speak with someone right away, or a staff member calls you back within one business day.

2

A few questions, then a package

Whether someone referred you, whether you have coverage, and what you need help with. Then a short package with rates, what to bring and the cancellation policy.

3

The appointment

Arrive 15 minutes early. Assessments are usually done in one day with breaks. Counselling starts with a session to understand what brought you in.

4

Feedback and a report

For assessments, the psychologist walks you through the results and how the conclusions were reached. You leave with a written report and practical recommendations.

Assessments that explain, not just label.

Every assessment ends the same way: a feedback meeting where the psychologist walks you through what was found and what to do about it, and a written report you can take to the school.

Outreach trips, for the communities that need them most.

British Columbia's geography, its mountains, islands and remote northern regions, creates real challenges for families seeking specialized psychological and educational services, and waitlists in large urban centres can stretch for months or years. Our clinicians travel with all testing materials and coordinate with local schools or community centres to create private, child-friendly testing environments, for psychoeducational, autism and school assessments.

Health authoritiesContracted by multiple health authorities across BC
School districtsResults provided to educators to inform Individual Education Plans
Community organizationsIndigenous communities, CYMH, child development centres and local non-profits

Rates

Please call for rates. Assessment fees are in the package you receive when you book; counselling fees depend on the clinician.

Coverage

Many extended health plans cover Registered Psychologists and Registered Clinical Counsellors. We issue receipts rather than billing insurers directly. Psychiatry is MSP-covered with a doctor's referral.

Cancellations

48 hours' notice, or the full session cost applies. Appointments are first come, first served. Hours are Monday to Friday, 8am to 5pm.

In an emergency, contact your family physician or an emergency department, or call 9-1-1. The clinic does not provide care after business hours. More questions answered
