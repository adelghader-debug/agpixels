At Compass Clinic, I provide psychological assessment services to children, adolescents, and young adults who wish to learn more about cognitive and emotional selves. I approach individuals through a warm, collaborative, and strengths-based stance. I aim to create a supportive environment where young people feel seen and understood, and where families can gain helpful insights to support their child’s growth and well-being.

My exposure into the world of psychological assessment began in 2018. I have since garnered experience assessing a wide range of concerns, including Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder, Specific Learning Disorders, Intellectual Developmental Disorder, Fetal Alcohol Spectrum Disorder, eating disorders, mood disorders, and anxiety disorders. Over the years, I have had the opportunity to be part of various organisations including BC Children’s Hospital, Child and Youth Mental Health, Pacific Community Resources Society, and Adler Community Health Services.

I am a Registered Psychologist in British Columbia and hold a Doctor of Psychology (Psy.D.) in Clinical Psychology from Adler University in Vancouver.
