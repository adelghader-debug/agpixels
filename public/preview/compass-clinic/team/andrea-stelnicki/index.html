I work with children, adolescents, and adults providing psychological assessment, consultation, psychoeducation, and intervention. I take a comprehensive, systems approach to work with young people and their families who are searching for an answer about why they might be struggling at school, home, work, or in their daily journey. I have experience working in public and rural schools, not-for-profit agencies, and mental health clinics with a wide range of clients of all ages and with unique strengths and areas of need.

I have extensive training and experience in the assessment of learning disorders, intellectual ability, attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder (ADHD), autism spectrum disorder (ASD), mood and anxiety disorders, language disorders, and other complex neurodevelopmental disorders. I completed my Ph.D. in Educational Psychology from the University of Calgary and I am a registered psychologist in BC & Alberta.
