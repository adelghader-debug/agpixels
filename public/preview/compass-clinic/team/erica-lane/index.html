I provide psychological assessments for children, adolescents, and adults, specializing in learning disorders, intellectual ability, ADHD, and mood and anxiety disorders. My goal is to help clients and their families understand the reasons behind challenges at school, home, or in daily life and to offer practical recommendations that provide support and guidance.

I aim to answer questions like, “Why is my child struggling in school?” or “Why is it so hard for me to focus?” by tailoring assessments to each individual’s needs and circumstances. My approach emphasizes collaboration, ensuring that families and clients feel supported and empowered throughout the process.

Before becoming a registered psychologist in Alberta and BC, I worked for five years as a school counsellor and psychometrician, conducting psychoeducational assessments in school settings. I completed a Master of Arts in Counselling Psychology at McGill University, where I later taught the Master’s-level Ethics course in Counselling Psychology.
