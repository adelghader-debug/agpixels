Outreach trips for child assessments

Bringing experienced clinicians directly to the communities that need them most.

Why outreach matters in BC

British Columbia's geography, its mountains, islands and remote northern regions, creates real challenges for families seeking specialized psychological and educational services. In many rural and Indigenous communities, access to pediatric psychologists, educational consultants and developmental specialists is limited, and waitlists in large urban centres can stretch for months or years. Compass Clinic's outreach trips help close that gap.

What we offer on a trip

Each trip is planned around the hosting community's needs. Depending on location and priorities, services may include:

  • Psychoeducational assessments for learning disabilities, ADHD, giftedness and academic challenges.
  • Autism assessments using the ADOS-2 and detailed developmental interviews.
  • School assessments requested or coordinated through schools, with results provided to educators and support teams to inform Individual Education Plans and classroom strategies.

Our clinicians travel with all testing materials and coordinate with local schools or community centres to create private, child-friendly testing environments.

Who we work with

  • Health authorities. Compass Clinic is contracted by multiple health authorities across BC.
  • School districts. Supporting students with learning, behavioural and developmental needs and planning interventions.
  • Community organizations. Indigenous communities, Child and Youth Mental Health, child development centres and local non-profits.

Before, during and after

Before each trip we work with local stakeholders to identify priority cases, plan logistics and ensure continuity of care. Afterwards we offer virtual debriefs, training workshops for local professionals, and joint care planning with school counsellors, speech-language pathologists and behaviour consultants. Teams typically complete up to 10 comprehensive assessments per week during a visit, depending on trip size and staffing. Each assessment can unlock school supports, funding eligibility (including autism supports) and clearer next steps.

Questions communities ask

How long does an assessment take?
Most comprehensive assessments take between 3 and 6 hours spread across sessions, plus report-writing time.
How much does it cost?
Costs vary by assessment type and community. Contact us for a quote and potential subsidy options.

Looking ahead

Compass Clinic is expanding outreach across British Columbia: new destinations, more frequent visits, and stronger community partnerships. We welcome inquiries from school districts, health authorities, non-profits and families who would benefit from a visit.
