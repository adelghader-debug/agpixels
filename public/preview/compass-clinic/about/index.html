About Compass Clinic

A multidisciplinary assessment and counselling clinic, with a public-private partnership that brings us the province's most complex cases.

Our mission is to help find your, or your child's, pathway to success.

Compass Clinic is a multidisciplinary counselling and diagnostic assessment clinic in Vancouver, with offices in Surrey and Nanaimo. We contract to numerous health authorities in the province and also see privately referred clients from across the Lower Mainland and beyond. The team provides complex assessment, consultation and treatment to clients of all ages who want to understand their own, or their child's, strengths and weaknesses.

Experienced clinicians

The team includes Registered Psychologists, Registered Clinical Counsellors, career counsellors, occupational therapists, speech-language pathologists and a psychiatrist. We support each other through confidential case conferences, mentorship and learning sessions.

Everything under one roof

If an assessment calls for occupational therapy or speech-language assessment, we can offer it in the same clinic. Clinical counsellors and psychologists both offer therapy here, so families have a range of options after a diagnosis.

Public and private

Our contracts with public health authorities mean we see some of the most complex cases in the province. We also take contracts from schools and Indigenous communities, and travel on outreach trips.

Clear communication

Complex diagnoses can raise anxiety across a whole family. We aim to deliver results in a clear, understandable and meaningful way, so that families can move forward with the right intervention.

Join the team

Compass Clinic actively recruits. Clinicians get supervision and mentorship, outreach opportunities across BC, residency and practicum training, flexible schedules, and an administrative team that handles intake, scheduling, scoring and billing so they can focus on clinical work.
