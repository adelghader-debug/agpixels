Questions families ask

Hours, booking, rates, coverage, cancellations and emergencies. If your question is not here, call and ask.

What times are you open?

Monday to Friday, 8am to 5pm.

Are you available after hours?

After-hours appointments are available for counselling with Registered Clinical Counsellors. Most psychoeducational assessments are completed during weekday office hours. Occasional and limited weekend availability may be possible, as only certain psychologists offer assessments in that slot. Please ask.

How do I schedule an appointment?

The first time you call, you will either speak with someone right away or a staff member will call you back within one business day. They ask a few basic questions: whether someone referred you, whether you have insurance, and what you or your child needs help with.

Once a date and time are set, we send a short package with our rates, how we communicate, what to bring, and how we handle cancellations. Appointments are made on a first come, first served basis.

How early should I arrive?

Please arrive 15 minutes early for a psychoeducational assessment or a first counselling appointment, so you can find the location and parking and complete paperwork.

What are your rates?

Please call for rates. Counselling fees depend on the clinician; assessment fees are included in the package you receive when you book. The fee for the video-based adult ADHD assessment is published on its service page.

Am I covered by insurance?

Many people have extended health insurance for services provided by a Registered Psychologist, and many plans also cover Registered Clinical Counsellors. We do not bill insurers directly; you receive a receipt to submit. Psychiatry is covered by MSP with a doctor's referral. Grants are available through the BC government for post-secondary students with learning disabilities who require updated testing.

Do I need a referral?

Only for psychiatry, which requires a referral from your doctor. Assessments and counselling can be booked directly.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please let us know 48 hours before your appointment. If you miss an appointment or cancel with less than 48 hours' notice, the full cost of the session applies.

What about emergencies?

We do not provide care after normal business hours. In an emergency, contact your family physician or an emergency department, or call 9-1-1.

What is the difference between a psychoeducational assessment and a LEAP assessment?

A psychoeducational assessment examines cognitive, academic, social and behavioural functioning and can be completed every two years. A LEAP academic assessment updates only the academic portion, about a year later, and requires a psychoeducational assessment first.
